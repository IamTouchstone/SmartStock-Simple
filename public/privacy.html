Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 29, 2026 • Application: SmartStock

Summary of Core Commitments

SmartStock collects basic profile information (email address, full name, and profile photo) when you log in via Google solely to authenticate your account and manage your retail store inventory. We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with third parties.

1 Application Overview & Contact Information

This Privacy Policy applies to the SmartStock mobile and web applications (hosted at https://smartstock-424c3.web.app). SmartStock is designed to provide retail store owners with fast, secure inventory management, barcode scanning, and stock level monitoring.

Application Name: SmartStock

Developer & Support Contact: bernieamce@gmail.com

2 Information We Collect

We believe in strict data minimization. We only collect information that is strictly required to provide and secure your SmartStock service:

  • Information from Google Login: When you choose to authenticate using Google Sign-In, we receive and store your:
    • Email address: Used as your primary account identifier and for critical account alerts.
    • Full Name / Display Name: Used to personalize your store profile and identify your tenant organization.
    • Profile Photo URL: Displayed within the app's profile screen for your visual convenience.
  • Store & Business Profile Details: Your designated store name and chosen accounting currency (e.g., NGN ₦, USD $, GHS ₵, KES KSh).
  • Inventory & Product Records: Product names, SKUs, barcode numbers, prices, cost figures, stock quantities, and categorization data entered by you to manage your business inventory.
  • Alternative Authentication Details: If you use email/password or phone verification instead of Google, we store your email address or phone number securely for authentication.

3 Why We Collect This Data (Purpose of Processing)

All data collected is utilized exclusively for legitimate business and operational purposes:

  • Account Creation & Authentication: To create your user profile, verify your identity, and safeguard your login sessions across mobile APKs and web browsers.
  • Inventory Management & Tenant Isolation: To ensure your product records, stock counts, and sales history are strictly separated and only accessible by you.
  • Operational Alerts: To notify you of low inventory levels, safety stock thresholds, and stock replenishment requirements.
  • Customer Support: To assist you with account verification, data recovery, and technical troubleshooting when you reach out to us.

4 Zero Data Sharing with Third Parties

SmartStock does NOT share, sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your personal information or store inventory data to any third parties.

We do not partner with data brokers, advertising networks, or external marketing companies. Your data remains strictly between you and your SmartStock cloud database.

5 Google API User Data Policy & Limited Use

SmartStock's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We only access Google user data with your explicit consent via Google OAuth.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to others unless necessary to provide or improve user-facing features.
  • We do not use Google user data for serving advertisements.
  • Humans are not permitted to read your personal Google data unless you explicitly give us consent for support purposes.

6 Data Security & Storage

Your data is stored in enterprise-grade Google Cloud Firestore infrastructure with robust encryption at rest and in transit via HTTPS/TLS protocols. Access rules are strictly enforced at the database level so only authenticated owners can access their respective organization records.

7 How to Request Data Deletion

You have full autonomy over your personal information and business records. You can request the complete deletion of your account and all associated data at any time:

Step-by-Step Deletion Request:

  1. Send an email from your registered Google address to: bernieamce@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: "Data Deletion Request - SmartStock"
  3. Include your store name or registered email in the body.

Upon receipt of your request, your account credentials, user profile, and all associated inventory and product records will be permanently and irreversibly purged from our databases within 48 hours.
